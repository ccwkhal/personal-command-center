Safe connection options for your personal Telegram account.

Signed in

Checking authentication status…

Connect Telegram identity

You can add Telegram's official Login Widget to verify which Telegram user is accessing your dashboard. Login does not grant access to private chats, contacts, account settings or the ability to send messages as your personal account.

About “full control”: Telegram does not provide a safe official website permission that gives a third-party dashboard unrestricted control of a personal account. I won't ask for your Telegram password, one-time login code, QR session, or session file. This starter dashboard therefore offers official links and can be extended with a Telegram bot you own for limited tasks such as sending notifications and managing that bot.

Possible official integration

  • Telegram Login Widget for identity verification.
  • Your own Telegram bot for alerts, reminders and budget notifications.
  • Bot administration for groups or channels where the bot has been granted appropriate rights.
